How can we help?

For help with Midnight Chess Club, a bug report or a privacy request, email us.

Contact support

Report a problem

Tell us your game version, device and operating system, what happened and what you expected. A screenshot can help. The game’s Settings menu can create a local Support report; review it before attaching it to an email. Creating a report does not send it automatically.

Never share profile credentials, private save files or unredacted diagnostic information publicly.

Your saves

Campaign, Vs AI, tutorial and Hot Seat can be played offline. Saves are local to the device or browser; general cross-device account linking is not available. Clearing browser storage or removing a save can permanently remove local progress.

Privacy and deletion

Read our privacy policy for the information the game stores and uploads. In version 0.19, Settings > Delete profile & server data opens a named confirmation. Choose Remove from this device for local-only removal, or Delete profile & server data for authenticated server erasure. Offline requests remain pending; keep the app and stored data until confirmation. Earlier versions may lack this control. Device/platform unlocks and separately retained historical backups/logs are not erased by that endpoint. Contact chess@mkla.dev for privacy questions, access or deletion requests. Do not send credentials; we will discuss an appropriate way to verify the request.
